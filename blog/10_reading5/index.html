Thoughts and Reflection

The Internet Has Borders

It was devasting to learn that Wix, one of the popular web design product in the market to date was put on the BDS. However kudos for the author in staying aware of the political associations and backing of the products and softwares that liter these blue screens and involuntarily support. A common theme that has been made evidently clear with readings like these is that websites and digital spaces are no longer the mysterious and anyonmous as it was painted and marketed as but rather infested with ethical, poltical, and philosophical ugendas. There simply is no such thing as digital neutrality. It really is scary, the more I read about it how entire countries are dependent on these infrastructures and how one black mirror episode can devastate their economic status.

I Know a Place

Having Roblox play a big part in my childhood I really enjoyed this reading as it came across as nostalgic. Especially because I too in my earlier naive adolenscence attempted to be a roblox game developer. Albeit, not very seriously, I just created my very own free world with the roblox studio software and played around with the landscaping and mob tools available at the time. I really appreciate reading how the author feel in love with my childhood game, challenging both themselves and the limits of the roblox's worlds and their aesthetics. Reminds me of how Roblox used to be enjoying and exploring the creative spaces that other people created instead of the overstaturated attention and time draining addicting games meant for distracting everything else in life but one repetitive objective after the next.

Alive Internet Theory

Honestly it was my first time hearing about the Dead internet theory and it hit me hard when reading that not only is there an ever growin amount of AI Slop content and accounts but also that it's evolved to the point that REAL people are arguing with fake people. I get what the author is saying that it is the modern day users that are actually using the AI tools and our fault that these spaces are now being polluted by AI slop, but there are still responsiblities that companies should uphold to ensure that such power has proper regulations so that it doesn't replace genuinue content from real people. I really enjoyed the playhtml website that the author shared in the reading, it truly gives the sense that folks on the internet are sitting next to me.
